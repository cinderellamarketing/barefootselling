Every referral captured in under a minute, every renewal conversation on time, and the Barefoot Selling stages built into the system itself.

1 Where things stand today▶

You run two businesses side by side. The broking business has just over 350 clients, took 192 referrals in the last twelve months, and has grown 32%, 48% and 60% over the last three years. Barefoot Selling is where you want to spend more of your time, with the online course, the book and the podcast all under way.

Acturis handles quoting, policies and claims, and it does that job. What sits around it has no home. Referrals arrive through BNI, WhatsApp and phone calls. Follow-ups live in your diary and your memory. The email telling a client their renewal is coming up goes out by hand, from Charlotte.

You were honest about how that plays out. The opportunities worth five grand get remembered. The ones worth a hundred pounds slip, and a referral who doesn't ring you back gets forgotten. If you were out of action tomorrow, nobody could pick up where you left off.

You've used HubSpot, Pipedrive, Trello and a little Salesforce, always because someone else needed the data. This would be the first system set up for you.

3 Built on the Barefoot Selling process▶

The system uses your language rather than Capsule's defaults.

  • Contacts are Lead, Prospect, Live, Lapsed or Networking, as you define them
  • The sales stages are Engagement, Meeting agreed, Killer questions and Solution presented
  • The killer questions sit on each opportunity as their own section: current insurer, renewal date, broker relationship and cover needed
  • Every contact records who referred them and the date of your last one to one
  • After the sale, a customer care checklist takes a client from neutral to advocate

You said it follows your exact model. You also said there are a few things you want to nuance once you've thought about them. Those go into the discovery session before anything is built.

4 Getting a referral in, fast▶

Your rule was that capturing someone had to take under a minute. In the demo, adding a person took about 20 seconds: name, company, phone, email, who referred them, and a note of what you talked about.

  • From your inbox, the Capsule add-on for Outlook or Gmail adds the sender as a contact and saves the email conversation to their record
  • From WhatsApp, the message is pasted into a note on the contact, where it is timestamped
  • Away from the desk, the mobile app does the same from your phone

Referred by builds the chain as you go. Open anyone and you can see who introduced them, and who introduced that person, all the way back to where it started.

5 Renewals and follow-ups you don't have to remember▶

On the call you mentioned a referral that came in that day, for a conversation in February about a renewal in April. At the moment that becomes a diary note you can't see from here.

In Capsule it becomes a dated task on that person's record, with the notes from the first conversation attached. When February comes, it appears in your calendar with the context beside it. Anything missed shows as overdue until it is dealt with.

For existing clients, a renewal checklist runs on each client record: a half-year review, a pre-renewal call and the renewal itself. With retention at 96 to 98%, renewals don't need to sit in a pipeline. The tasks do the job.

6 What goes in the pipeline, and what it's worth▶

New business only. Renewals stay on the client record as tasks, so the insurance pipeline holds new business and what you see there is genuinely new income.

Income, not premium. Each opportunity carries one value. You record the income you expect from it, for example £250 on a £1,000 policy, and refine it as the deal moves through the stages. Each stage carries a probability, so Capsule also shows a projected value across the whole pipeline.

That matters for your targets. At the moment you hear where you stand against the group's income target about twice a year. With a goal on your home screen, you see it every day. Goals and opportunities need to use the same measure, which is another reason to settle on income.

7 Renewal emails without the manual work▶

The email that tells a client their renewal is coming up and asks them to book a meeting goes out one at a time. Transpond, Capsule's sister email tool, sends it from the same data.

  • Your client list stays in Capsule. Transpond is only the tool that sends
  • You filter in Capsule, for example every client renewing in December, and send to that group
  • Once it is set up, the renewal email can be automated rather than sent one by one

You said the system is starting to creak under the number of referrals and the size of the book. Automating the routine emails is how the service stays where it is as both grow.

8 Your client data▶

Your renewal list, with every existing client and their renewal date, is imported in bulk at the start. Nobody retypes it.

The data in Capsule is yours. It can be exported at any time as a spreadsheet, so your client list is never held only inside someone else's system. What, if anything, comes out of Acturis beyond your renewal list is for you and the group to agree.

2 The points that shaped this proposal▶

The value is income. Premium is the only figure you have before the quote, but income is what the group targets you on. Recording expected income per opportunity, and refining it, keeps the pipeline and your goals on the same measure.

Renewals are not opportunities. With retention at 96 to 98%, new business is what needs nurturing. Renewal care runs on the client record with its own checklist: half-year review, pre-renewal and renewal.

For insurance, aftercare can move onto the client record too, which leaves the Customer care board free for coaching clients.

The renewal email is the first thing worth automating, because referral volume and the size of the book are both growing and it currently goes out by hand.
